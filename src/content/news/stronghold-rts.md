Firefly Studios' *Stronghold* launched in October 2001 as a real-time strategy game for Microsoft Windows and Mac OS X, originally published by Gathering of Developers. The title became the first installment in the Stronghold series and achieved commercial success, surpassing 1.5 million units sold globally by 2004. A remastered Definitive Edition followed on 7 November 2023, released for Windows by Firefly Studios two years after the studio's acquisition by Devolver Digital.

## Gameplay & Campaign Structure

*Stronghold* casts the player as a lord in a medieval kingdom whose geography and nobility closely resemble Medieval England. The core objective is constructing a stable economy and a capable military to defend territory, assault enemy castles, and complete mission parameters.

The game offers two primary campaigns. The military campaign tasks the player with reclaiming a kingdom divided among four rival lords — Duc de Puce (The Rat), Duc Beauregard (The Snake), Duc Truffe (The Pig), and Duc Volpe (The Wolf) — after the king is captured abroad. The player character, whose father was killed in an ambush, receives support from two loyal lords, Lord Woolsack and Sir Longarm. Mid-campaign, the king returns upon ransom and grants the player command over his armies for the final assault on Volpe's fortress.

An economic campaign set after the military campaign focuses on rebuilding portions of the kingdom, with goals tied to obstacles such as bandits and fire. Additional modes include single-mission combat and economic scenarios, alongside single-player and multiplayer support.

## Legacy & Community

The original release earned positive reviews from outlets including PC Gamer and GameSpy. The community maintained long-term engagement through an in-game map editor and scenario creator. The game's popularity prompted multiple sequels, and the original received a high-definition re-release in 2013 before the 2023 Definitive Edition remaster.
