Supergiant Games shipped *Hades II*, its first sequel, as a roguelike action RPG across two platform waves. The game launched September 25, 2025 on macOS, Windows, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2, with PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S versions following on April 14, 2026. Early access began on Windows in May 2024 and macOS in October 2024. Greg Kasavin directed, with art by Jen Zee and a score from Darren Korb and Austin Wintory.

## Plot & Protagonist

Players control Melinoë, Princess of the Underworld and sister to Zagreus, the protagonist of the 2020 original. Chronos, the Titan of Time, returns after being overthrown by his children, seizes the Underworld, and imprisons Hades, Persephone, and Zagreus. Hecate escapes with the newborn Melinoë to the Crossroads in Erebus, raises her, and trains her for revenge. Melinoë descends to Tartarus with Olympian Boons to defeat Chronos, though Chronos uses his time powers to resurrect after each defeat. The narrative eventually splits into an Underworld path and a Surface path, with Melinoë coordinating with a past-timeline Zagreus via Nyx's Mirror to destroy Chronos permanently and slay Typhon.

## Core Mechanical Updates

Like the original, *Hades II* is a roguelike dungeon crawler with primary attacks, weapon-specific specials, and a dash. New systems include a revised magic "cast" that locks enemies in place and a "Magick Bar," a combat resource that recharges through attacking. Olympian Boons return, offering three persistent choices per encounter. The game adds Hexes—summon attacks tied to the goddess Selene, enhanced via a per-run skill tree.

## Structure & Progression

The sequel introduces two distinct routes. The Underworld path runs through Erebus, Oceanus, the Fields of Mourning, and Tartarus, with bosses Hecate, Scylla, Cerberus, and Chronos. The Surface path covers the City of Ephyra, the Rift of Thessaly, Mount Olympus, and the Summit, with bosses Polyphemus, Eris, Prometheus, and Typhon. Each region unlocks after clearing the previous one.

The Crossroads serves as the overworld hub. Players spend run-derived resources to upgrade abilities, craft incantations that introduce new features, and unlock weapons. NPC bonds and romance options progress alongside the plot. "God Mode" returns, granting increasing damage resistance per failed run—20% base, plus 2% per failure, capped at 80%.
