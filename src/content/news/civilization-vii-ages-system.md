Firaxis Games has officially detailed the structural overhaul driving *Sid Meier's Civilization VII*. The strategy title restructures human history into three distinct eras: Antiquity, Exploration, and Modernity, each featuring unique mechanics, resources, and building types.

## Era Transitions & Leader Decoupling

- **Age Transitions**: At the conclusion of each Era, players select a new civilization based on historical paths or fulfilled unlock criteria while retaining their chosen Leader persona.
- **Independent Commanders**: Military units stack under designated Commanders who accumulate experience, skill points, and combat bonuses across campaigns.
- **District Expansion**: Urban tiles expand organically across adjacent terrain hexes, removing worker micro-management in favor of direct city planning menus.

## Engine & Performance Enhancements

The updated graphics engine supports full 4K procedural terrain rendering with real-time seasonal weather shifts. Cross-platform multiplayer and cross-progression are supported at launch.
