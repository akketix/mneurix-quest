Age of Empires IV arrives on PlayStation 5 on November 4, 2025, marking the first time the title appears on a Sony platform. Developed by Relic Entertainment in partnership with World's Edge and published by Xbox Game Studios, the real-time strategy game previously launched on Windows on October 28, 2021, and on Xbox One and Xbox Series X/S on August 22, 2023. It is the fourth installment in the Age of Empires series and the first not developed by Ensemble Studios.

## Civilizations & Expansions

The base game, known as Age of Empires IV: Anniversary Edition, includes ten civilizations. Eight were available at original launch — Abbasid Dynasty, Chinese, Delhi Sultanate, English, French, Holy Roman Empire, Mongols, and Rus — with Ottomans and Malians added free on October 25, 2022. The total roster now stands at 23 civilizations across the base game and multiple expansions. The Sultans Ascend added six civilizations, including four variant civilizations that modify existing rosters. Knights of Cross and Rose added two variants, Dynasties of the East added four, and Yue Fei's Legacy added one. A subsequent expansion, Raiders of the North, introduced Vikings and Scots.

## Campaigns & Age System

The game features seven single-player campaigns, two of which are DLC-exclusive. Console versions include a tutorial campaign, The Rise of a King. Other campaigns cover the Norman conquest, the Hundred Years War, the rise of Moscow, the Mongol Empire, The Sultans Ascend, and Yue Fei's Legacy. The game uses four Ages — Dark, Feudal, Castle, and Imperial — with advancement handled by constructing Landmarks rather than at the Town Center, with certain civilizations such as the Knights Templar and Abbasid Dynasty using modified systems.

## Technical Details

Age of Empires IV runs on the Essence Engine 5.0 and supports both single-player and multiplayer modes. Directors Adam Isgreen and Quinn Duffy led development, with a composing team including Tilman Sillescu and Mikolai Stroinski among others. No minimum or recommended hardware specifications were provided for the PlayStation 5 release.
