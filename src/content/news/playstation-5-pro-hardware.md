Sony Interactive Entertainment released the PlayStation 5 Pro on November 7, 2024. As a mid-generation hardware refresh, the Pro model introduces a faster GPU, improved ray tracing capabilities, and AI-driven upscaling technology to the PlayStation 5 family.

## Hardware Specifications
The PlayStation 5 Pro is powered by a custom AMD 8-core Zen 2 CPU with a maximum clock speed of 3.85 GHz. Its graphics processor utilizes a mixed architecture based on AMD Radeon RDNA 2 and RDNA 3, incorporating RDNA 4 RTRT cores. This GPU configuration features 60 compute units (CUs) with a maximum clock speed of 2.35 GHz, achieving a peak performance of 18.05 TFLOPS.

In addition to the 16 GB GDDR6 SDRAM found in all PS5 models, the Pro model includes an extra 2 GB of DDR5 RAM dedicated to system processes. Storage capacity is doubled compared to the base launch model, featuring a 2 TB custom integrated PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD. Users can further expand storage up to 8 TB via an M.2 NVMe PCIe Gen4 x4 port.

## Connectivity and Form Factor
The Pro model upgrades wireless connectivity to Wi-Fi 7, replacing the Wi-Fi 6 standard used in the base and Slim revisions. It retains Gigabit Ethernet, Bluetooth 5.1, and multiple USB ports, including three USB 3.2 Gen 2x1 ports and one USB 2.0 port.

Display output supports resolutions up to 8K, an addition over the 4K maximum on previous models. The console maintains the same supported framerates and includes an HDMI 2.1 port. Physically, the PlayStation 5 Pro measures 388 × 216 × 89 mm and weighs 3.1 kg. It launched with an introductory price of US$699.
