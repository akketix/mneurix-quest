Funcom's *Dune: Awakening* launched for Windows on June 10, 2025, with early access beginning June 5 for Deluxe and Ultimate Edition purchasers. The survival MMO sold one million copies within two weeks of its PC release. PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S versions are scheduled for September 22, 2026.

## Setting & Narrative

The game is set in an alternate *Dune* timeline where Paul Atreides was never born. Lady Jessica obeyed the Bene Gesserit and gave birth to a daughter, Ariste. Her loyalty earned her training as a Truthsayer, allowing her to expose Dr. Wellington Yueh's treachery and prevent the events of the original novel. House Atreides survives under Duke Leto's leadership and enters a protracted War of Assassins with House Harkonnen on Arrakis. The Fremen have vanished following a Sardaukar purge ordered by Emperor Shaddam IV.

Players control an undercover Bene Gesserit agent tasked with finding the Fremen and waking "the Sleeper."

## Survival & Progression Mechanics

Players create a custom avatar, select a background, and choose a mentor who determines their starting ability. A Bene Gesserit mentor, for example, teaches the use of "the Voice" to taunt enemies. Core survival systems require managing thirst and maintaining stillsuits. Arrakis presents environmental hazards including sandstorms, quicksand, heat stroke during daytime exploration, and hostile Sardaukar patrols at night.

Players scavenge resources to craft items and build shelters, with outpost construction requiring tax payments to the Emperor. Spice harvesting and transport is a competitive activity central to the game's economy. Players also become addicted to spice, unlocking related powers as they progress.

Sandworms pose a constant threat. Players must minimize noise to avoid attracting them. Thumpers can draw sandworms to the player's advantage, but they cannot be summoned at will. Items and vehicles consumed by sandworms are permanently lost, though general progress is retained on defeat.

## Multiplayer Structure

The game progresses through four phases: Survive, Protect, Expand, and Control. Players advance from an ordinary survivor to a guild leader. After the starting area, players access the Overland, using ornithopters from a top-down view to travel between locations.

Players can join House Atreides or House Harkonnen for faction-specific quests. The Deep Deserts zone supports hundreds of players in a primarily PvP environment, with weekly Coriolis storms reshaping the map and revealing new points of interest. Underground ecology labs function as dungeon content for solo or group play. The endgame involves guilds using influence at the Landsraad to make decisions affecting the game world.

## Development & Post-Launch Content

Funcom developed *Dune: Awakening* using Unreal Engine 5, with NUKKLEAR co-developing vehicular gameplay. The first content update, Chapter 2, and its accompanying DLC, *The Lost Harvest*, released on September 9, 2025, expanding the player's origin story and adding a Dicycle ground vehicle.
