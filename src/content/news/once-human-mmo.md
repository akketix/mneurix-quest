Once Human is a free-to-play post-apocalyptic survival game developed and published by Starry Studio, a subsidiary of NetEase. Built on the NeoX engine, the game blends third-person shooter and looter shooter mechanics within a shared sandbox open world. It was released for Windows on July 9, 2024, followed by iOS and Android on April 23, 2025, with PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S versions scheduled for August 25, 2026.

## Gameplay Systems
Players load into a shared environment and complete a tutorial that unlocks survival systems and progresses the narrative. From the main menu, players select a story-driven scenario, such as Manibus, and join a server. Servers operate on a timed shelf life and wipe after the time expires. Progression is tied to memetics, a skill system where players unlock skills using ciphers earned through quests and leveling.

A key structural element is Eternaland, an isolated island where players store high-end items, gear, and Deviants. These stored items can be carried into new scenarios, though transfer limits are based on item rarity. In Eternaland, players can build using dust, a material generated from inventory items that cannot be carried over.

Survival mechanics require players to manage hunger, hydration, and sanity meters. Maintaining hunger and hydration above certain thresholds grants buffs to health and damage. Spending time in supernatural areas decreases the sanity meter, reducing the player's maximum health pool. Sanity can be restored by consuming specific resources or resting in a bed.

## World Structure and Combat
The open world features strongholds, silos, and monolith battles. Strongholds are large, themed locations populated with enemies and resources. Activating a stronghold's rift anchor yields rewards and progresses the player toward monolith battles. Monolith battles are multi-phase boss encounters, of which there are currently three unique variations. Silos function as instance-generated dungeons with multiple difficulty tiers, populated by basic and elite enemies, and culminating in a mini-boss encounter.

## Plot and Expansions
The narrative begins with an amnesiac player waking up after the Starfall, an event caused by the alien substance Stardust that mutated most life into violent Deviations. The player is a Meta-Human, a biologically enhanced being created by the company Rosetta, who caused the Starfall. Players can collect friendly Deviations, known as Deviants, to assist in combat or resource gathering.

In September 2024, Starry Studio released The Way of Winter expansion. This scenario introduces an icy northern region where players reunite with Mitsuko, a first-generation Meta-Human, and encounter a mysterious masked figure named Igna.
