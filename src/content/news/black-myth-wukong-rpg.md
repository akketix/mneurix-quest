Black Myth: Wukong is a 2024 single-player action role-playing game developed and published by Chinese studio Game Science using Unreal Engine 5. Released for PlayStation 5 and Windows on August 20, 2024, and scheduled for Xbox Series X/S on August 20, 2025, the title sold 20 million units in its first month.

## Development & Commercial Performance
Directed by Feng Ji, Black Myth: Wukong is the first installment in the Black Myth series. The narrative and setting are inspired by the classical Chinese novel Journey to the West. Players control the Destined One, a staff-wielding monkey seeking six relics corresponding to Sun Wukong's six senses. The setting integrates real-world cultural and natural scenery, including the Kaiyuan Temple, Wulong Temple, and the Dazu Rock Carvings.

## Combat Mechanics
The game operates from a third-person perspective and incorporates elements of the Soulslike subgenre. Combat centers on a staff that extends and shrinks, utilizing smash, pillar, and thrust stances. Resource management dictates engagement: landing light attacks and well-timed dodges builds Focus, enabling heavy attacks. Stamina is depleted by sprinting, dodging, and attacking.

## Abilities & Equipment
Spells are divided into mysticism, alteration, strand, and transformations. With the exception of transformations, spells consume mana and have cooldowns. Transformations allow the protagonist to shapeshift into creatures with unique movesets and health pools, active until health or might is depleted. Additionally, spirits captured from defeated yaoguai chiefs and equipped vessels provide passive bonuses and active skills fueled by qi.

## Progression & Structure
Structured into six chapters across distinct locations, the game follows mostly linear progression interspersed with expansive areas. Keeper's shrines serve as checkpoints. The difficulty cannot be manually adjusted. Skill points, known as sparks, can be freely reallocated to adjust playstyle. Completing a playthrough unlocks a New Game Plus mode and a separate boss battle mode.
