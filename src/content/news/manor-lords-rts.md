Manor Lords, the medieval city-builder and real-time tactics game from Slavic Magic, launched into early access on April 26, 2024 with immediate commercial success. The title sold over one million copies during its launch weekend and surpassed three million total sales by February 2025. Developed by Grzegorz "Greg" Styczeń and published by Hooded Horse, Manor Lords overtook Hades II shortly before release to become the most-wishlisted game on Steam, accumulating over three million wishlists by launch.

## Gameplay & Systems

Set in late 14th-century Franconia, Manor Lords blends organic city-building with real-time tactical warfare. The city-building component avoids fixed zoning grids, instead allowing houses to be placed along curved roads shaped by trade routes and terrain. Players manage supply chains, trade networks, and seasonal demands, while a financial system layers in six types of taxation, imports and exports, and environmental factors such as soil exhaustion and deforestation.

The game ships with five modes: Rise to Prosperity (combat-free), Restoring the Peace, On the Edge, A Duel, and Fractured Realm. The remaining modes include varying degrees of combat, from bandit skirmishes to territorial expansion against AI lords and a boss encounter against an expanding Baron. Real-time battles factor in fatigue, weather, and equipment, with troops sourced through rented mercenaries, a trained retinue, or armed villagers. A diplomacy system lets players influence AI lords through threats or influence.

## Development & Post-Launch Support

Development was partly funded through Patreon donations and a 2021 Epic MegaGrants award. Originally built on Unreal Engine 4, the project announced a switch to Unreal Engine 5 in June 2024, with the migration completed by August 22 in version 0.7.987.

After a nine-month content hiatus, a major overhaul update arrived on October 2, 2025. The patch reworked internal code and gameplay at large, adding two new game modes, four new maps, and a revamped perk system.
