Total War: Warhammer III is a turn-based strategy and real-time tactics game developed by Creative Assembly and published by Sega. Released on February 17, 2022, it is the third entry in the Total War series set in Games Workshop's Warhammer Fantasy universe, following Total War: Warhammer (2016) and Total War: Warhammer II (2017). The game is available on Linux, macOS, and Windows.

## Core Gameplay

The game follows the established Total War formula, combining turn-based campaign management with real-time tactical battles. Players move armies and characters across a campaign map, manage settlements, engage in diplomacy, and fight AI-controlled factions. When armies collide, battles play out in real time. A custom battles mode lets players create tailored real-time engagements, and online multiplayer is supported.

The main campaign spans from the Lands of the East to the Realms of Chaos. Game director Ian Roxburgh stated the campaign map is roughly twice the size of the Eye of the Vortex campaign map from Total War: Warhammer II.

## Factions & Races

Playable races include the human civilizations of Grand Cathay (based on Imperial China) and Kislev (influenced by Slavic cultural archetypes), along with five Chaos factions. Four of these are devoted to individual Chaos Gods — Khorne, Tzeentch, Nurgle, and Slaanesh — while the Daemons of Chaos faction is led by a customizable Daemon Prince whose traits evolve through "Daemonic Glory" earned during the campaign. The Ogre Kingdoms race was made available as a bonus to early adopters who pre-purchased or bought the game within the first week of release.

## Immortal Empires

Owners of the first two games gain access to "Immortal Empires," a combined world map that merges content across the trilogy. Similar to the "Mortal Empires" campaign in Total War: Warhammer II, this mode launched as a Beta on August 23, 2022 alongside patch 2.0. Players who own races from the earlier titles also unlock those races for multiplayer use in the third game.

## Campaign Narrative

The prologue follows Yuri Barkov, a Kislevite commander sent to find the missing Bear-God Ursun. Yuri is corrupted by Chaos during his expedition, ultimately killing his brother Gerik and renouncing Ursun. After wounding the god, Yuri is transformed into a Daemon Prince. The main campaign centers on the Advisor, now enslaved by the Tome of Faces, who seeks a drop of Ursun's blood to free himself, guiding various factions into the Realm of Chaos to reach the imprisoned god.
