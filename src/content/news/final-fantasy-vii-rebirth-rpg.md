Final Fantasy VII Rebirth, the second entry in Square Enix's planned trilogy remaking the 1997 PlayStation classic, is now available on Nintendo Switch 2 and Xbox Series X/S as of June 3, 2026. The action role-playing game was developed by Square Enix Creative Business Unit I and originally launched on PlayStation 5 on February 29, 2024, followed by a Windows release on January 23, 2025.

## Release Timeline & Platforms

The multiplatform rollout spans roughly two and a half years. The PS5 version served as the initial release, with the Windows port arriving roughly eleven months later. The Switch 2 and Xbox Series X/S versions mark the final confirmed platforms, bringing the game to all current-generation hardware. A third entry, Final Fantasy VII Revelation, is set to conclude the remake trilogy in 2027.

## Gameplay & Structure

Rebirth picks up immediately after the events of Final Fantasy VII Remake (2020), following the party's escape from Midgar. The game shifts away from the linear progression of its predecessor toward a more open-ended overworld. Objective markers in the heads-up display track main story quests, sidequests, and their distances from the party. Traversal options include both foot travel and Chocobo mounts.

Combat retains the hybrid real-time action and Active Time Battle (ATB) system from Remake. Players freely switch between party members during combat, executing physical attacks, magic, and items through a pause-based menu. The "Synergized" mechanic, originating from the "Episode INTERmission" DLC for Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade (2021), returns and enables synchronized attacks between party members.

## Narrative Scope

The story covers events from the departure from Midgar through the Forgotten Capital, though the order of locations differs from the original 1997 release. Notably, the Wutai segment is reserved for the third installment. The core conflict follows Cloud Strife and AVALANCHE opposing the Shinra Electric Power Company's exploitation of the Planet's Lifestream while confronting Sephiroth.

## Critical Reception

Upon release, Rebirth received critical acclaim and was nominated for and won multiple end-of-year awards, including Game of the Year. The lead development staff from Remake returned, with Tetsuya Nomura shifting to creative director and Naoki Hamaguchi assuming the director role.
