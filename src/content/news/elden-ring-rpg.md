Elden Ring, the 2022 action role-playing game directed by Hidetaka Miyazaki with worldbuilding by George R. R. Martin, is officially coming to Nintendo Switch 2. Developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment, the Switch 2 version is scheduled for release on August 28, 2026. The game originally launched on February 25, 2022 for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X/S.

## Core Gameplay & World Design

Elden Ring is a third-person action RPG set in an open world called the Lands Between. Players control a customizable character on a quest to repair the Elden Ring and become the new Elden Lord. The world comprises six main areas, including Limgrave's grassy plains and the undead-infested wasteland of Caelid. Players traverse the environment using their steed, Torrent, and can fast travel between checkpoints called sites of grace outside of combat.

Combat includes melee weapons, ranged attacks, magic spells, shields, and stealth mechanics. Players can dodge, block, or parry enemy attacks, with parries and stealth enabling critical hits. Weapons can be modified using Ashes of War, which grant special abilities called weapon arts. Runes serve as the in-game currency for leveling attributes, purchasing items, and upgrading gear. Death drops all held runes at the location of death, and a second death before retrieval permanently loses them.

The game also features hidden dungeons, catacombs, tunnels, and caves where players fight bosses and collect items. An online multiplayer mode supports cooperative boss fights and player-versus-player combat.

## Sales, Reception, & Future Projects

Elden Ring sold over 30 million copies and won several Game of the Year awards, earning recognition as one of the best-selling and most acclaimed games of all time. The downloadable content *Shadow of the Erdtree* launched in June 2024 and sold over ten million copies. A multiplayer-focused spinoff, *Elden Ring Nightreign*, released in 2025. A film adaptation is currently in production, slated for 2028.
