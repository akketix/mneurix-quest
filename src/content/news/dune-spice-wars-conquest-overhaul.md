Shiro Games and Funcom have deployed a major patch for *Dune: Spice Wars*, expanding the strategic depth of Conquest Mode with faction-specific Hero units and updated Landsraad voting cycles.

## Tactical & Political Overhauls

- **Hero Units**: Factions can deploy specialized operational commanders (such as Duncan Idaho or Glossu Rabban) featuring active tactical skills and regional buffs.
- **Spice Tax Mechanics**: Dynamic market pricing governs spice exchange rates, forcing players to balance imperial tax obligations against CHOAM stock buyouts.
- **Deep Desert Navigation**: Worm sign detectors and airfield supply lines reduce attrition damage when marching armies across open sand sectors.

## Balance & Multiplayer Updates

Ranked 4-player free-for-all lobbies now include spectator tools and automated pause management during critical Landsraad resolutions.
